Data Deletion — Verso

App: Verso — PDF Reader & AI Tools (package com.adylabs.verso)
Developer: AdyLabs · Contact: adylabs.android@gmail.com

Verso has no user accounts — the app uses an anonymous identifier. This page explains how to delete the data associated with that identifier and the data stored on your device.

Delete your server-side data (in the app)

  1. Open Verso.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This permanently deletes the server-side data linked to your anonymous identifier: AI usage counters, server-side records, and any AI content reports you submitted. This action cannot be undone, and your free-tier usage history will start fresh.

Delete your on-device data

  • Uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Verso → Storage → Clear storage. This removes documents stored in the app's private storage, saved chats, preferences, and the app lock setting.
  • Files you exported or saved to shared storage (e.g., your Downloads folder) remain yours and are untouched — delete them with your file manager if you wish.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app option (for example, you already uninstalled the app), email adylabs.android@gmail.com with the subject "Verso data deletion request". Note that because identifiers are anonymous, we may be unable to locate your records without information from your device; we will respond within 30 days.
