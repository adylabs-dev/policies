Privacy Policy — Verso

App: Verso — PDF Reader & AI Tools (package com.adylabs.verso)
Developer: AdyLabs · Contact: adylabs.android@gmail.com
Effective date: June 12, 2026

This policy explains what data Verso collects, why, and what control you have over it. The short version: your documents live on your device, you never create an account, and the only document content that ever leaves your device is the text you explicitly send to the AI features.

1. No account required

Verso has no sign-up and never asks for your name, email address, or any other contact details. To protect our AI service against abuse and to enforce fair-use limits, the app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your identity and that we cannot use to identify you.

2. Your documents

  • PDFs and other files you open, scan, edit, merge, split, compress, protect, sign, watermark, or redact are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded for these operations.
  • Text recognition (OCR) and document scanning run on-device using Google ML Kit. No image or text leaves your phone for OCR.
  • The app lock (PIN/biometric) is handled by your device. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never visible to us.

3. AI features (Chat, Flashcards, Quizzes, Summaries, Translation)

When you explicitly use an AI feature, the relevant document text (and your prompts) is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS) to our backend (Firebase Cloud Functions on Google Cloud), which forwards it to Google's Gemini models to generate the response.

  • Content is sent only when you initiate an AI action — never in the background.
  • We do not use your documents or conversations to train AI models.
  • Requests are protected by Firebase App Check (Play Integrity), so only genuine installs of the app can reach the service.
  • We store usage counters (number of AI requests) keyed to your anonymous identifier to enforce free-tier and fair-use limits.
  • If you save an AI chat to your library, it is stored on your device. If you report an AI response (for example as harmful or incorrect), the reported conversation excerpt is transmitted and stored so we can review it.
  • AI output is generated automatically and may be inaccurate. Do not rely on it for legal, medical, or financial decisions.

4. Advertising (free version)

The free version of Verso shows ads served by Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, no ad request is made until you have made a choice in the consent dialog (Google UMP). Depending on your consent, AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device information to serve and measure ads — see Google's privacy & terms. You can change your consent choice at any time in the app's settings, and you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Verso Pro and the ad-free upgrade remove all advertising.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage entitlements; RevenueCat receives your purchase token and the app's anonymous user ID — see the RevenueCat privacy policy.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand aggregate feature usage and to fix crashes. This involves pseudonymous identifiers, device model, OS version, and event/crash data. It never includes the contents of your documents.

8. Data retention and deletion

Server-side data tied to your anonymous ID (usage counters, saved server-side records, content reports) is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my data. This removes the server-side data associated with your anonymous identifier.
  • By email: write to adylabs.android@gmail.com.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data (your documents in the app's storage, saved chats, preferences).

See the dedicated data-deletion page for step-by-step instructions. Records that Google Play keeps about your purchases are governed by Google's own policies and are not deleted by us.

9. Security

All network traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Backend endpoints require a valid Firebase App Check attestation from a genuine app install. Server-side databases are not publicly accessible.

10. Children

Verso is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold almost no personal data and cannot identify you from an anonymous ID, the most effective controls are the in-app deletion tool and the consent settings described above. For anything else, contact us and we will help: adylabs.android@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
